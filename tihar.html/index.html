Festival of lights:Tihar

Tihar Festival is one of the most beautiful festivals in Nepal, celebrated with lights, flowers, and happiness. It honors crows, dogs, cows, oxen, and the special bond between brothers and sisters. Homes are decorated with oil lamps and colorful rangoli, spreading joy and positivity. Tihar teaches love, gratitude, and harmony among people and nature.
